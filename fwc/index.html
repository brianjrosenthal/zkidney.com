The free water clearance (CH2O) is

The formula is Free water = CH2O=V X (1-Uosm/Sosm)

If the free water clearance is negative, it means that the kidney is concentrating urine while if it is positive it means the kidney is diluting urine.These functions keep us in balance during hot days, or when we overingest water, but in circumstances where the urine is abnormally concentrating water, the serum sodium level may fall (hyponatremia). This condition is known as the syndrome of inappropriate antidiuretic hormone (SIADH). ADH is a hormone associated with urine concentration, but is also increased with stress, certain medications, and cancer. Over dilution can likewise cause hypernatremia. Alternatively the free water clearance can be calculated by measuring the serum and urine sodium and the serum potassium (K).

V X (1-{(UNa+UK)/Serum Na})
