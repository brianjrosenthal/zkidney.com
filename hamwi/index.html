FRAME

ADJUSTED BW = ACTUAL DRY WEIGHT +((STANDARD BW - EDEMA FREE BW) *25)

This value is calculated from frame size, height, and gender based on Standard body weights in NHANES 2 data. Refer to Frames & Nhanes

Reference: Frisancho AR. New standards of weight and body composition by frame size and height for assessment of nutritional status of adults and the elderly. Am J Clin Nutr. 1984 Oct;40(4):808-19.

  1. Adjust body weight by frame, height, and gender
  2. Look up body frame from NHANES2 tables
  3. Calculate frame from height and wrist circumference
