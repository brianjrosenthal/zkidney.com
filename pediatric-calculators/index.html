CKiD SCHWARTZ EQUATION (Scr,BUN,Cystatin C)

GFR = 39.1*[ht/Scr].516
*[1.8/cystatin C]0.294
*[30/BUN]0.169
*[ht/1.4]0.188
*1.099(if male)

  • GFR= Glomerular filtration rate in cc/min/1.73m2
  • Ht = Height in meters
  • Cystatin C = mg/L
  • Scr = Serum creatinine = mg/dL
  • BUN = mg/dL
BEDSIDE SCHWARTZ EQUATION (CREATININE)

GFR=0.413*Ht/Scr

Ht=Height in centimeters

COUNAHAN-BARRATT EQUATION (CREATININE)

GFR=0.43*Ht/Scr

Ht=Height in centimeters
